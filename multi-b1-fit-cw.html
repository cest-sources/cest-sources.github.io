Multi-B1 Z-spectra fitting

Several studies showed that by using multiple B1 irradiation amplitudes the non-linear interaction of exchange rate and B1 in R1ρ can be employed to fully quantify an exchanging system.

Dowload zipped Matlab implementations here or find the package on github.com/cest-sources/multiB1_fit_Z-cw_2pool
